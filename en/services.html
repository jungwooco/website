Services

Sea and air freight forwarding at the core, with customs and inland transport coordinated as one flow — door to door.

Ocean FreightFCL / LCL

We handle FCL and LCL sea import/export. We compare carrier schedules and rates on each route to offer competitive terms, and manage everything from booking and shipping documents (B/L) to delivery at destination.

  • FCL import/export
  • LCL import/export
  • Schedule & rate comparison
  • B/L & shipping documents

Air FreightTIME-CRITICAL

For time-critical, small-volume or high-value cargo. We compare airline options on both rates and schedules, and manage timing from pickup so that airport cut-off times are never missed.

  • Air import/export
  • Urgent cargo
  • Schedule management

Customs SupportKOREA CLEARANCE

Working with licensed customs brokers, we support Korean import/export clearance. HS codes and regulatory requirements are checked in advance, and transport and clearance are coordinated through a single point of contact — no double follow-up needed.

  • Import/export clearance
  • HS code check
  • Schedule coordination

Inland & MoreWITHIN KOREA

From port or airport to the final destination in Korea. We manage container pick-up and return schedules together with demurrage and detention timing, and arrange cargo insurance where needed.

  • Inland trucking
  • Demurrage & detention
  • Cargo insurance

Dangerous Goods ExportDG · CHEMICALS

From UN class verification and MSDS review to DG packing, labelling, declaration and carrier approval — we handle the full DG export process. With extensive experience in mixed chemical products across multiple classes, we keep shipments from being delayed by documentation issues.

  • Chemical products
  • UN classification
  • MSDS review
  • DG packing & labelling
  • Carrier approval

Food & Agri ImportQUARANTINE MANAGED

We handle food and agricultural imports into Korea, mainly from Southeast Asia and China. Since quarantine and import declaration drive the overall schedule, we coordinate everything from shipping schedules to customs clearance together with licensed customs brokers.

  • Food quarantine
  • Agri quarantine
  • Import declaration
  • SE Asia & China
  • Schedule management
