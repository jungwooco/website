About Us

A licensed freight forwarder handling sea and air import/export cargo since 2008.

How we work is who we are

JUNGWOO SEA & AIR is a freight forwarding company providing global logistics services for sea and air import/export cargo. On behalf of our clients, we negotiate transport terms with carriers and airlines, and manage the process from booking through shipping documents to delivery at destination.

We do not handle only general cargo. Dangerous goods exports involving chemical products across multiple UN classes, food and agricultural imports subject to strict quarantine, door-to-door export across North America — the more complex the procedure, the more experience we have accumulated.

We do not predefine the scope of our work. Whether it is an HS code check or coordination with a local partner, if our client needs it, we handle it together. Schedules and costs are confirmed before we proceed, and any change is shared immediately.

Rather than overstating our scale, we have kept to one principle since 2008 — handle the cargo we are entrusted with, accurately.
